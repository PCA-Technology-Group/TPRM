========================================
ASSESSMENT
========================================

ASSESSMENT
----------
AssessmentId (PK)
ClientVendorId (FK -> ClientVendor)

(FK -> ApplicationUser, nullable)

ClientAcknowledgedDate
(nullable)

...


PURPOSE:

The central business object.

Represents:

"Client X is assessing Vendor Y."


PERMISSION RULE:

Netrio Admin:

May initiate.

Netrio Analyst:

May initiate.

Client Admin:

May request an Assessment but cannot directly
initiate one.

Client Viewer:

Cannot initiate.

Vendor Contact:

Cannot initiate.


NOTE:

The exact persistence mechanism for a Client Admin's
Assessment request has not been modeled yet.


Client and Vendor are reached through:

Assessment
    -> ClientVendor
        -> Client
        -> Vendor


TierIdSnapshot preserves the Tier used when the
Assessment was created.


ScoringConfigIdSnapshot preserves the applicable
scoring configuration.


Assessment does not directly store OverallScore.

Score history is stored in AssessmentScore.


CLIENT REVIEW:

Client Review is read-only.

The Client reviews the score, Findings, and
Recommendations.

The Client acknowledges the Assessment.

ClientAcknowledgedBy records who acknowledged it.

ClientAcknowledgedDate records when acknowledgment
occurred.


CLOSURE:

Once the Assessment reaches Closure, the entire
Assessment becomes immutable.

After Closure:

No new Responses.

No new Evidence may be attached to Responses.

No new Reviews.

No Finding changes.

No new score calculations.

No questionnaire scope changes.

No acknowledgment changes.

AssessmentQuestions for the MVP.


========================================
QUESTION EXCLUSION
========================================

Questions are not physically deleted from an
Assessment.

They are marked as excluded.


AssessmentQuestion:

IsExcluded = true

ExcludedBy = User

ExcludedDate = timestamp

ExclusionReason = reason


EXCLUDED QUESTIONS:

Are not shown to Vendor

Cannot receive Responses

Do not require Review

Do not participate in scoring

AFTER DISTRIBUTION:

Added

Removed

Excluded

Restored

Reworded

Reweighted


The question scope becomes immutable when the
Assessment is distributed.


PERMISSION RULE:

Questionnaires may be sent by:

Netrio Admin

the distribution event.


========================================
RESPONSE
========================================

RESPONSE
--------
ResponseId (PK)
AssessmentQuestionId (FK -> AssessmentQuestion)
Answer
SubmittedBy (FK -> ApplicationUser)
SubmittedDate
...


PURPOSE:

Stores a Vendor response to an AssessmentQuestion.


AUTHORIZED CREATOR:

Vendor Contact.


RESPONSES ARE APPEND-ONLY.


A submitted Response is never modified.

A changed answer creates a new Response.


========================================
CURRENT RESPONSE
========================================

The Response with the newest SubmittedDate is the
current Response.


Conceptually:

SELECT TOP 1 *
FROM Response
WHERE AssessmentQuestionId = X
ORDER BY SubmittedDate DESC


A new Response requires a new Review.

An old Review does not apply to the new Response.

========================================
FLAGGED RESPONSE WORKFLOW
========================================

The MVP has no back-and-forth chat system.


Response v1:

Review = Flagged

Comments =
"Please provide a current penetration-test report."


Vendor does not edit Response v1.

Vendor submits Response v2.

Response v2 requires a new Review.

All historical Responses and Reviews are preserved.


========================================
REVIEW COMPLETION
========================================

An Assessment cannot proceed to Scoring until every
included AssessmentQuestion's current Response has
a terminal Review status.


TERMINAL:

Accepted

Rejected


NON-TERMINAL:

Not Reviewed

Flagged


Excluded AssessmentQuestions are ignored.


A new Response makes the AssessmentQuestion
unresolved until the new Response is reviewed.

========================================
FINDING
========================================

FINDING
-------
FindingId (PK)

AssessmentQuestionId
(FK -> AssessmentQuestion)

CreatedFromReviewId
(FK -> Review)

ResolvedByReviewId
(FK -> Review, nullable)

Title
Description

Recommendation

Severity

Status

CreatedDate
ResolvedDate

...


PURPOSE:

Represents a risk identified from a Rejected
Response.


MVP RELATIONSHIP:

One Rejected Response produces one Finding.

Findings are not consolidated across multiple
questions for the MVP.


FINDING CREATION:

When the current Response receives:

Review.Status = Rejected

the system automatically creates a new Finding.


The Finding is associated with:

- The AssessmentQuestion that failed
- The Rejected Review that created the Finding


NEW FINDING:

Status = Open


TITLE:

Automatically generated from the rejected
AssessmentQuestion.


DESCRIPTION:

Automatically generated from the rejected
AssessmentQuestion / Response.


RECOMMENDATION:

Written manually by Netrio Analyst or Netrio Admin.


SEVERITY:

Stored on the Finding.

The mechanism used to determine Severity has not yet
been defined.


========================================
FINDING RESOLUTION
========================================

A Finding is never deleted merely because the Vendor
later fixes the issue.


Example:

Response v1

    ↓

Review = Rejected

    ↓

Finding #1

Status = Open


Vendor submits Response v2.

Response v2:

    ↓

Review = Accepted

    ↓

Finding #1

Status = Resolved


When resolved:

ResolvedByReviewId points to the Accepted Review.

ResolvedDate records when the Finding was resolved.


The original Finding remains permanently for audit
history.


========================================
REPEATED FINDINGS
========================================

A Resolved Finding is never reopened.


Example:

Response v1

    ↓

Rejected

    ↓

Finding #1 = Open


Response v2

    ↓

Accepted

    ↓

Finding #1 = Resolved


Response v3

    ↓

Rejected

    ↓

Finding #2 = Open


Finding #1 remains Resolved.

Finding #2 represents the new occurrence.


========================================
FINDINGS & RECOMMENDATIONS COMPLETION
========================================

Every Open Finding must have a Recommendation before
the Assessment may move from:

Findings and Recommendations

to:

Client Review


Example:

Finding #1

Status = Open

Recommendation =
"Require MFA for privileged accounts."

READY


Finding #2

Status = Open

Recommendation = NULL

NOT READY


Resolved Findings do not block progression to
Client Review.


========================================
FINDING FREEZE
========================================

When the Assessment enters Client Review, all Findings
become immutable.


During Client Review:

Title cannot change.

Description cannot change.

Recommendation cannot change.

Severity cannot change.

Findings cannot be deleted.


No separate IsLocked field is required.

The rule is enforced based on Assessment.Status.


========================================
CLIENT REVIEW
========================================

Client Review is read-only for the MVP.


The Client reviews:

- Assessment score
- Findings
- Recommendations


The Client does not:

- Edit Findings
- Reject Findings
- Approve individual Findings
- Request Finding changes through a workflow


The Client acknowledges the Assessment.


Acknowledgment is stored on Assessment:

ClientAcknowledgedBy

ClientAcknowledgedDate


After acknowledgment, the Assessment may move to
Closure.


========================================
RESOLVED FINDINGS
========================================

Resolved Findings remain visible in the final
Assessment history and eventual report.


The Client can therefore distinguish:

OPEN FINDINGS:

Risks that still exist.


RESOLVED FINDINGS:

Risks identified during the Assessment that were
subsequently remediated.


Resolved Findings are not deleted or hidden.


========================================
ASSESSMENT CLOSURE
========================================

Once the Assessment reaches Closure, the entire
Assessment becomes read-only.


After Closure:

No new Responses.

No new Evidence attached to Responses.

No new Reviews.

No new Findings.

No Finding changes.

No score recalculations.

No questionnaire scope changes.

No Client acknowledgment changes.


Historical records remain available for audit and
reporting purposes.
